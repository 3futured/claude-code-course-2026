# Week 4 Reflection

## Where I land on using AI

Before this course I had no idea how Claude AI actually worked.
I'd used it to chat, but never as a tool for building something real.
Going through devlens changed that. I saw how you can have a real 
conversation with it about code, get unstuck, and actually ship something.

I'd use it to learn faster, explore new technologies, and understand 
what's happening under the hood, not just copy-paste answers, but 
dig into why things work the way they do.

## One thing I understand now that I didn't at the start

GitHub. I'd never touched it before this course. Now I understand 
what it actually is a place where code lives, changes are tracked, 
and you can see the full history of a project. That felt like pulling 
back a curtain. And it made me want to keep going to get closer 
to how programming actually works, not just the surface of it.
